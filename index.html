QuickBooks Online Integration

C13 Inc

This page describes the QuickBooks Online integration operated by C13 Inc, an accounting and bookkeeping firm. If you reached this page from QuickBooks Online, the information below explains what the integration does and how to disconnect it.

This is internal software. It is used by C13 personnel in the course of engagements C13 performs for its clients. It is not offered to the public, is not available for sign-up, and there is no account to create.

What it does

Some accounting systems record a customer payment in QuickBooks Online without applying it to the invoice it pays. The invoice then continues to show an open balance even though the money has been received. This integration finds those unapplied payments and applies them to that same customer's open invoices, oldest invoice first.

Where a payment is larger than the invoices available to it, the excess is left unapplied and reported for manual review rather than allocated. Matching is performed strictly within a single customer. The integration does not process funds, and does not create, void, or delete invoices or payments.

What it accesses

  • Invoices — customer, date, amount, outstanding balance
  • Payments — customer, date, amount, unapplied balance
  • Customer records — identifier and display name only
  • Company name, used to confirm which company is connected

It does not access payroll, employee data, bank or card numbers, or tax identification numbers, and holds no permission to process payments. Data is processed on computers controlled by C13 and is not transmitted to any third party. Full detail is in the privacy policy below.

How to disconnect

A QuickBooks Online company owner or administrator can disconnect this integration at any time, from within QuickBooks Online:

  • Open Settings (the gear icon)
  • Under Apps, choose Connected apps
  • Find this integration and choose Disconnect

Disconnection takes effect immediately and prevents any further access. Payment applications already recorded remain part of your own accounting records and stay under your control.

3. What the Connector does

At a staff member's request, the Connector reads reports, lists and transactions from QuickBooks Online and creates or updates accounting records, such as journal entries, bills, invoices and the recording of payments, and asks QuickBooks to email invoices, estimates and sales receipts. A staff member reviews a preview of each change and approves it before it is sent. The Connector does not delete or void records and does not move money or process payments. Staff use it through Claude, an AI assistant provided by Anthropic; see the Privacy Policy for how information is handled.

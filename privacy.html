Privacy Policy

C13 Inc · Effective August 8, 2026

This policy describes how C13 Inc ("C13," "we," "us") handles data accessed through its QuickBooks Online integration, an internal application used solely in the course of accounting and bookkeeping engagements C13 performs for its clients.

The application is not offered to the public

This application is built and operated by C13 for its own use in client engagements. It is not distributed, sold, or licensed to third parties, and it is not available for general sign-up. It connects only to QuickBooks Online companies whose owner or authorized administrator has explicitly granted access through Intuit's authorization process.

What data the application accesses

When connected to a QuickBooks Online company, the application reads, through Intuit's Accounting API:

  • Invoices, including customer reference, transaction date, amount, and outstanding balance
  • Payments, including customer reference, transaction date, amount, and unapplied balance
  • Customer records, limited to the identifier and display name needed to group transactions
  • Basic company information, used to confirm which company is connected

It does not access payroll records, employee data, bank account or card numbers, tax identification numbers, or payment card information. It does not request or hold any scope permitting payment processing.

What the application does with that data

The application identifies payments that have been recorded in QuickBooks Online without being applied to an invoice, matches them to that same customer's open invoices on a first-in, first-out basis, and writes the resulting application back to the corresponding payment record. Matching is performed strictly within a single customer; data is never compared or combined across customers or across companies.

Where data goes

Accounting data retrieved from QuickBooks Online is processed on computers controlled by C13 and is written only to storage controlled by C13. It is not transmitted to any third party, and specifically:

  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking, or advertising services are used
  • No third-party hosting, cloud processing, or subprocessors are involved
  • Data is never sold, rented, licensed, or shared for marketing purposes
  • No data is used to train machine learning models

Communication with Intuit occurs over encrypted connections (HTTPS) using Intuit's published API endpoints.

Credentials

Authorization tokens issued by Intuit are stored in a local file on the C13 computer that performs the work, outside any shared or synchronized folder. Tokens are not transmitted elsewhere and are not shared between personnel; each person who operates the application completes their own authorization.

Retention

Reports generated by the application contain client financial information and are retained as part of the engagement record in accordance with C13's records retention practices, currently three years. Authorization tokens are retained only while the connection is active and are discarded when access is revoked.

Revoking access

A QuickBooks Online company owner or administrator may disconnect this application at any time from within QuickBooks Online, under Settings → Apps → Connected apps. Disconnection takes effect immediately and prevents any further access. Data already written to the company's QuickBooks records — that is, the payment applications the application created — remains part of the company's own accounting records and is under the company's control.

Your data in QuickBooks Online

Data held within QuickBooks Online is governed by the agreement between the company and Intuit, and by Intuit's privacy statement. This policy covers only C13's handling of data accessed through the integration.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the effective date above will be updated and connected clients will be notified in the ordinary course of their engagement.

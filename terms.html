3. What the Application does

The Application identifies customer payments recorded in QuickBooks Online that have not been applied to an invoice, and applies them to that same customer's open invoices on a first-in, first-out basis. Where a payment exceeds the invoices available to it, the excess is left unapplied and reported for manual review rather than allocated. The Application does not create, void, delete, or alter invoices or payments beyond recording these applications, and does not process funds.

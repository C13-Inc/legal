Privacy Policy: C13 Accountant Connector

C13 Inc. Effective 11 November 2026.

1. What this policy covers

This policy describes how the C13 Accountant Connector (the "Connector") handles information from QuickBooks Online. The Connector is an internal application that C13 Inc ("C13") built and operates for its own client engagements: accounting, bookkeeping, financial statement preparation, payroll and advisory work. It is not sold, licensed or offered to anyone else, and there is no public sign-up.

2. Who uses it, and on which companies

Only C13 personnel use the Connector, signed in with their C13 Microsoft accounts. It connects only to a QuickBooks Online company whose owner or administrator has granted access through Intuit's authorization screen. A C13 administrator makes each connection. A C13 administrator assigns each staff member the client companies they work on, and the Connector refuses any other company. A staff member with no assignment cannot reach any company.

3. What it reads and writes

When a staff member asks it to, the Connector reads company information and preferences, reports (such as profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow, trial balance, general ledger and aging reports), lists (such as accounts, customers, vendors, employees, items, classes and terms) and transactions (such as invoices, bills, payments, estimates, journal entries, deposits and expenses), and attachments.

It creates and updates accounting records, such as journal entries, bills, invoices, payments received and bill payments, customers and vendors, as part of C13's work. Before any change is sent, the Connector shows the staff member a preview of it in the chat and the staff member approves it; C13's Claude settings then require a separate approval click before the change is posted. The Connector refuses a change that does not match a current preview made under the same staff member's sign-in. The Connector does not delete or void records, does not move money, and does not use QuickBooks Payments. Bill payments are recorded only.

At a staff member's request, the Connector also asks QuickBooks to email an invoice, estimate or sales receipt to the address on that record, using QuickBooks' own email service.

The Connector reads and updates the employee list (names and contact details). QuickBooks Payroll data, such as payroll runs and pay stubs, is not available to it.

3a. Information about people

Client records can include names, addresses, email addresses and phone numbers of a client's customers, vendors and employees. For C13 staff, the Connector uses their Microsoft sign-in name and account identifier to record who made each change.

4. Where the information goes

  • Intuit. The Connector exchanges data with Intuit's QuickBooks Online services over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Microsoft Azure. The Connector runs on Microsoft Azure App Service in the United States (West US 3). Its encryption key is held in Azure Key Vault. Staff sign in through Microsoft Entra ID.
  • Anthropic. C13 staff use the Connector through Claude, an AI assistant provided by Anthropic under C13's Claude Team plan. When a staff member asks Claude a question, the QuickBooks information the Connector returns for that question is sent to Claude so it can answer. Anthropic processes it under its Commercial Terms of Service, which state that Anthropic may not train models on customer content from its services. Those terms do not commit to a location for this processing, so it may take place outside the Azure region named above. Claude conversations are kept in C13's Claude workspace until they are deleted. Anthropic removes a deleted conversation from its back-end systems within 30 days, except that it may keep content flagged by its trust and safety systems for up to two years (and the related classification scores for up to seven years), and may keep data where the law requires. Disconnecting a company from the Connector does not delete conversations. C13 has turned off the setting that lets staff send chat feedback (ratings) to Anthropic.

C13 operates the Connector for its own engagements and does not process information on Intuit's behalf. C13 does not sell QuickBooks information, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it beyond the providers named above, except as the client directs or the law requires. The Connector includes no analytics, advertising or tracking services.

5. What the Connector keeps

  • Authorization tokens for each connected company, encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key held in Azure Key Vault. When a C13 administrator disconnects a company, the Connector cancels them with Intuit and deletes them. When a company is disconnected inside QuickBooks, Intuit stops accepting them at once; the Connector then marks the company as disconnected, and deletes the tokens when a C13 administrator removes the company from the Connector.
  • A write log and recovery records of changes staff made: who made and confirmed the change, when, to which company, the record type, its QuickBooks ID and total, and, for email address changes and emails sent, the addresses involved. These are kept with C13's engagement records, currently for three years after the engagement ends.
  • C13's own settings: which clients C13 works for and the rules that limit what staff may change in each company.

Apart from the write log and recovery records above, the Connector does not store the reports or transactions it reads. Claude conversations are covered in section 4. Work papers that staff prepare from that information are kept with C13's engagement records under C13's records retention practices, currently three years after the engagement ends. The Connector's own service logs record operational events, such as sign-ins, errors and which company a request was for; they do not record the contents of reports or transactions.

6. Security

The Connector accepts only HTTPS connections (TLS 1.2 or later). Staff sign in with C13 Microsoft accounts that require multi-factor authentication. Tokens are encrypted at rest. The Connector's own outbound connections go only to Intuit's QuickBooks and sign-in services and Microsoft's sign-in and Azure services; it refuses other addresses.

7. Disconnecting

A company's owner or administrator can disconnect the Connector at any time from the Manage integrations page in QuickBooks Online. The Connector can no longer reach the company once Intuit processes the disconnection. A C13 administrator can also disconnect a company. Changes already made to the company's records stay in its QuickBooks records.

7a. Requests about information

A client, or a person whose information appears in a client's records, may ask what the Connector keeps about them, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted, by writing to info@c13inc.com. C13 will respond as its engagement agreements and applicable law require. Records in QuickBooks itself are held by the client and Intuit.

8. Changes to this policy

C13 will update this policy when the Connector's handling of information changes, and will change the effective date when it does.
